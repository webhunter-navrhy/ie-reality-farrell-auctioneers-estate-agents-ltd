Description

Farrell Auctioneers, Valuers & Estate Agents are delighted to bring to the market this pretty bungalow excellently located within walking distance to Kinvara village. The dwelling itself was built in c. 1973 and extends to approx. 1,200 sq ft and is an E energy rating. It sits on a corner site and the garden has many varieties of mature shrubs and trees and a beautiful fuchsiahedging, has a block built wall to sides of the house, a brick wall to the front with a single iron gate with a path leading to the light filled porch. There is a separate double iron gate providing access to the side of the house with parking and another double iron gate providing access to sheds and the rear of the property. The land at the rear has a stone wall boundary and iron farm gate for access.

Its accommodation includes a large bright entrance porch, entrance hallway, kitchen / dining room, living room, sitting room, 4 bedrooms and a bathroom.

There are steps down to yard and sheds to the rear and a ramp and hand--rail at side door, The property has wall lights and a seating area on raised concrete plinth beside back door

Shed measurements:

Shed 1 280 x 400, galvanised roof, work bench, window and door with lock

Shed 2 450 x 320 galvanised roof over open area for refuse bins

Shed 3 560 x 400 galvanised roof, shelves workbench and double iron doors with lock and 150 cm lean-to shed

This property is ideally located within easy walking distance to the seaside village of Kinvara and its renowned cafés, restaurants, shops, schools, picturesque quay and all local amenities, the property also enjoys an excellent location just a 30-minute drive from Galway City, with easy access to the spectacular Burren National Park and the world-famous Wild Atlantic Way.
